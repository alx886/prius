👋 Welcome Gen 2 Prius Driver!

This companion was custom engineered to solve the #1 design quirk of the 2nd Generation Prius: guessing how much fuel you actually have left!

Because the Gen 2 contains a flexible rubber fuel bladder in the tank, its capacity fluctuates constantly, shrinking in the winter cold and expanding in summer heat. Your dashboard gauge often jumps up and down unpredictably.

How it works:

  1. When you log a fill-up, the tool calculates your true capacity by cross-analyzing pump volumes.
  2. As you save logs, the Fuel Check module checks your historic bladder capacity baseline for the current weather to deliver a highly accurate safety-first Distance-to-Empty (DTE) diagnostic.

🔒 100% Private: I do not collect or transmit any of your logs or location metrics. Everything is saved strictly on your local device.
